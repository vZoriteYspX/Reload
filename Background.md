# Charge
__Overview__\
Charge, also known as Reload, is a clapping game of unknown origins. Its first mention online was in 2022. However, its existence can go as far back as past 2015.

__Preface__\
Back when I was in grade 2, there was a new clapping game that swept through the upper batches of school's high school. From there, it slowly spread towards the lower batches until nearly everyone knew how to play the game.\
Additionally, there were even spin-offs of the game. This included a Fornite version, and a Pokemon version --- though said versions never really went mainstream in my school due to their complexity.\
\
Charge's popularity went on until the Covid-19 pandemic struck and everyone had to shift to online classes. Since we no longer saw each other physically because of quarantine/lockdown, and had no medium to play Reload, the game slowly faded away from school memory.\
\
With all of that said, this is my attempt at recreating my childhood game in C#. I no longer remember the exact rules, but I will try my best to preserve as much of the original as much as possible.
