# Reload

__Overview__
Charge, better known as Reload in the Philippines, is a clapping game of unknown origins. Its first mention online was in 2022. However, its existence can go as far back as past 2015.

__Preface__
Back when I was in grade 2, there was a new clapping game that swept through my school's high school batches. From there, it slowly crept to the lower batches until nearly everyone knew how to play the game. 
This went on until the Covid-19 pandemic struck and everyone had to shift to online classes. Since we no longer saw each other physically because of lockdown, and with no medium to play Reload, the game slowly faded away from school memory.

With all of that said, this is my attempt at recreating my childhood game in C#. I no longer remember the exact rules, but I'll try my best to preserve the original as much as possible.
